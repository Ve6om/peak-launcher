### New
- Servers tab: save the servers you play on, see who's online, ping and the server message, and join with any instance in one click. You can also import the servers saved in an instance.
- Sign in with your browser, or with a code or QR code from another device
- Your skin: player heads on your accounts and a 3D view of your skin on the Accounts page
- Performance flags in Settings: None by default, or Smooth (G1), Aikar's flags and ZGC. Each instance can pick its own.
- Instances show their mod loader's logo when they don't have modpack art

### Fixed
- Stop and Kill now always end a running game. Before, a hiccup right after launch could leave the game running with no way to stop it from Peak.
