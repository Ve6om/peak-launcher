### New
- Modpack instances show whether they're on the latest version: "Latest" or "Outdated" next to the version, in the library and on the instance page
- Peak no longer pops up about modpack updates when it starts. It asks when you press Play on a pack that has an update: update first, play anyway, skip that version, or stop asking for that pack.

### Fixed
- After Install & play on a server, the button could stay on "Installing…" even though the pack had finished, and Peak didn't join the server
- If a pack still needs files downloaded by hand, Peak no longer tries to join the server with mods missing; it tells you to finish the downloads and press Play
