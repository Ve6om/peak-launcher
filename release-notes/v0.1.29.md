### New
- Share a log: the console and the crash window can upload the log to mclo.gs and copy the link, for asking for help. Before it's uploaded, Peak replaces your home folder, Minecraft name and sign-in token, and asks you to confirm since the link is public.
- What's new: each mod in the update list has a button that shows the changes in the new version, from Modrinth or CurseForge.

### Improved
- The console filter highlights what it matched, shows how many lines match, and opens with Ctrl+F (Esc clears it).
