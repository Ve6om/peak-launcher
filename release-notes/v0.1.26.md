### New
- Worlds tab: every instance now lists its single-player worlds with their game mode, version, when they were last played and how much space they take. Play straight into a world, rename it, make a copy, copy its seed, open its folder or move it to the recycle bin.
- World backups: back up any world with one click and restore it later. Restoring backs up the world as it is first, so it can be undone, and works for worlds that were deleted too.
- Automatic backups: turn on "Back up worlds when the game closes" (in the Worlds tab or Settings → Storage) and Peak backs up each world you played, keeping the newest few. Backups you make by hand are always kept.
- Data packs: add or remove a world's data packs from the Worlds tab.
