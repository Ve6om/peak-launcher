### New
- Modpack updates: when a modpack you installed has a new version, Peak tells you when it starts. You can update right there, skip that version, or tell Peak not to ask about that pack again.
- Change a modpack's version without making a new instance. Open the instance's Settings and pick any version under Modpack, newer or older. Your worlds, settings and screenshots stay, and your old mods folder is backed up in the instance's backups folder so mods you added yourself aren't lost.
- Each modpack has its own "Update reminders" switch, and there's a "Tell me about modpack updates" switch in Settings to turn the reminders off everywhere
- Reset all settings: a button at the bottom of Settings puts everything back to the defaults. Your instances, accounts and worlds aren't touched, and your own CurseForge key is kept.
