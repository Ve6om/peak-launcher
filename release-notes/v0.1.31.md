### New
- Languages: Peak now speaks German, Spanish, French, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian and Chinese (Simplified). It follows your computer's language by default; change it under Settings → General → Language. Some messages from the game and from mod sites stay in English.
- Streamer mode: hides your account name, UUID, server addresses, folder paths and login details in the console, so you can stream or share your screen safely. Turn it on in Settings or press Ctrl+Shift+S. A pill in the title bar shows when it's on; click it to turn it off.
