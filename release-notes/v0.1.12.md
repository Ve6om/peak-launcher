### New
- Discord status: your profile shows Playing Peak Launcher with what you're doing, like the modpack you're playing and the server you're on. Turn it off in Settings, under Integrations.
- Your own logo and banner for any instance: Settings, then General
- Each instance has a Servers tab: the same list as Minecraft's Multiplayer screen, with who's online, Join, reorder and remove
- Joining a server from Peak also adds it to the game's own server list, so you can rejoin from inside Minecraft after a disconnect
- Star servers in My servers to keep them at the top, and move them up or down
- Install & play asks first, then shows the install while it happens and joins the server when it's ready. If the server runs a specific pack version, Peak installs that version, and offers to switch an instance that's on a different one.
- Your cape shows on your skin on the Accounts page
- Library cards show the modpack's logo, and Jump back in shows the instance's banner or cover art
- A Discord button in Settings, About

### Fixed
- Some CurseForge modpacks (like Prominence II) showed a screenshot or their mod loader's logo instead of their own logo
- Modpacks in Discover showed their mod loader's logo instead of their own
