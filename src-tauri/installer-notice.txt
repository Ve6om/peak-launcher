PRIVACY

Peak has no accounts on our servers, no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no crash reporting. Your settings, instances and sign-in stay on this computer, and your Microsoft sign-in is saved encrypted.

To do its job, Peak connects to:
- Microsoft, Xbox Live and Mojang: signing in, and downloading the game and Java.
- Modrinth, CurseForge (through our proxy at api.peaklauncher.com), Feed The Beast and Technic: when you browse or install mods and modpacks.
- GitHub: checking for a new version of Peak. You can turn this off in Settings, under Network.
- Servers you add to the Servers tab: to show their status.

Full privacy policy: https://peaklauncher.com/privacy/
